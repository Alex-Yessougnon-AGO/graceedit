# 01 — Product Brief

## Vision
Make professional-looking video creation accessible to people who do not know video editing.

> Pick your video. Tell GraceEdit what you want. Review the result. Share it.

## Positioning
GraceEdit should not be marketed internally as “a CapCut clone”. Its product identity is:

> A video-creation assistant with a professional editor underneath.

## Target users
### First-time creator
Low editing knowledge and possibly low digital literacy. Wants one successful video without training.

### Everyday creator
Comfortable with a smartphone, but not an editor. Wants fast social content.

### Church/media operator
Creates sermons, testimonies, worship clips, announcements, Bible verses and invitations.

### Advanced creator
Needs precise timeline, multi-track editing, effects, keyframes, audio and export control.

## Jobs to be done
- Turn a phone recording into a shareable video.
- Make a sermon easier to watch.
- Add subtitles without typing everything.
- Make the picture brighter and the voice clearer.
- Create a short version from a long recording.
- Prepare a video for WhatsApp/TikTok/Instagram/YouTube.
- Keep professional control when needed.

## Differentiation
1. Outcome-first UX.
2. Beginner-safe workflows.
3. Progressive disclosure.
4. Intelligent recommendations.
5. Offline-first editing.
6. Recipes/templates.
7. Jev-powered decision layer.
8. Professional Studio underneath.

## North-star metrics
- First Successful Video Rate.
- Time to First Successful Video.
- Export Success Rate.
- Beginner Task Completion.
- Recovery Rate after mistakes.

## Non-goals for the first stages
Do not start with a social network, collaboration, generative video, marketplace, complex 3D effects or cloud-only editing.

## Product principles
- Do not make users learn the editor before creating.
- Use safe defaults.
- Preview important changes.
- Make automation reversible.
- Explain technical concepts only when necessary.
- Keep basic editing local.
- Do not remove professional control; progressively hide it.

## Expanded product vision
GraceEdit can eventually become a **creation operating system for individuals, churches, creators, businesses, communicators and designers**. Video editing remains a core capability, but the same project can contain video, images, audio, text, layouts, scripts and generated assets.

### Future creative jobs
- Create a professional church announcement poster from a short brief.
- Turn a sermon into a short video, captions, thumbnail and social poster.
- Generate a campaign concept, script, storyboard, clips, voice and graphics, then assemble them.
- Create a YouTube thumbnail that follows a selected visual identity.
- Adapt one campaign into Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube and print-oriented formats.
- Give a user a design starting point based on their industry, audience, message and brand.
- Let a designer use AI as an art director/assistant without losing manual control.

### Product identity
The long-term product is not simply an AI generator. It is:
> **An intelligent creative studio that understands the user's objective, proposes a visual direction, creates or finds the required assets, composes them professionally, and lets the user refine the result.**

### Quality principle
GraceEdit should distinguish:
1. **Generation** — create raw material.
2. **Direction** — decide style, hierarchy, composition and audience fit.
3. **Composition** — place and size elements coherently.
4. **Refinement** — typography, spacing, color, contrast, crop, rhythm and consistency.
5. **Adaptation** — produce variants for different formats.

This separation is essential to avoid generic AI-looking designs.
