# 17 — Creative Studio Specification

## Purpose
The Creative Studio is GraceEdit's long-term expansion from video editing into multi-format content creation. It should help a beginner create professional communication materials while giving experienced creators editable, precise control.

## Core concept
```text
Intent
  ↓
Creative Brief
  ↓
Creative Direction
  ↓
Template OR Generation OR User Assets
  ↓
Composition
  ↓
Quality Review
  ↓
Variants
  ↓
Export
```

## Creation modes
### Quick Create
User gives a simple request. GraceEdit handles most decisions and presents a few meaningful choices.

### Guided Create
User answers questions about audience, message, style, format and brand.

### Studio
User edits layers, typography, media, timing, effects and design rules manually.

## Supported creative outputs
- Video
- Short/reel
- Poster
- Flyer
- Social post
- Story/status
- Thumbnail
- Image
- Audio/voice
- Scenario/script
- Storyboard
- Campaign
- Future: presentation/UI concept

## Creative brief schema
A brief should capture: objective, audience, message, domain, platform, format, duration, tone, visual direction, brand, required assets, forbidden elements, language and call-to-action.

## Domain intelligence
Domains can include church/ministry, education, business, events, fashion, food, technology, real estate, sports, personal brand, non-profit and more. Domain packs provide vocabulary, common layouts, visual conventions and content requirements, but users remain free to override them.

## Quality philosophy
Professional design is evaluated by hierarchy, clarity, typography, composition, spacing, contrast, consistency, relevance and restraint. “More effects” is not considered better.
